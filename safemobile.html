Safer mobile experiences, designed around privacy.

SafeMobile is our research initiative exploring a privacy-conscious Android security experience. We are investigating device risk insights, financial safety awareness and on-device intelligence.

SafeMobile is not yet released. No real-time antivirus, threat removal or financial protection service is currently being offered.

Device security research

Exploring useful ways to present device health and risk information clearly.

Privacy-conscious design

Investigating data minimisation, transparency and on-device processing where practical.

Financial safety awareness

Researching approaches that may help users recognise suspicious patterns and make informed choices.

On-device AI exploration

Evaluating whether local intelligence can assist security insights while limiting data movement.
