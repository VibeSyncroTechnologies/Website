Frequently Asked Questions

Is it free?

SafeMobile is still in development. Pricing and availability have not been announced.

Does it collect any data?

The design goal is a zero-telemetry, privacy-first architecture. Actual practices will be documented before release.

When will it launch?

No launch date is confirmed. The project will progress through research and private beta evaluation before a public launch is considered.
