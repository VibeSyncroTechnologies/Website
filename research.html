Research & Development

SafeMobile Antivirus & AI Initiative

Our R&D direction explores how security and intelligent systems can help people use mobile technology with greater confidence and control.

In Development

SafeMobile Antivirus for Android

SafeMobile is a planned Android security product focused on helping users understand device risks and protect sensitive digital activity. The research direction includes real-time security checks, safeguards for financial interactions and privacy-conscious processing. We are also exploring how on-device AI may support useful analysis while reducing unnecessary data sharing. SafeMobile is not yet released.

Research areas under consideration

  • Real-time vulnerability and device-risk scanning
  • Financial transaction safety and risk awareness
  • Zero-telemetry architecture as a design objective
  • On-device AI research for privacy-conscious assistance

Artificial Intelligence R&D

We are exploring AI technologies that can make digital products more helpful, responsive and context-aware. Our interests include practical AI experiences and approaches that keep security, transparency and user choice in view.

Why it matters

Innovation should strengthen people, not make them surrender control. Our motto — Building & Connecting People with Privacy & Security — is a reminder to evaluate new ideas through the lens of trust, responsible design and real user needs.
