Who We Are

VibeSyncro Technologies Pvt Ltd is an India-based technology company focused on Android application development and publishing, research in artificial intelligence, and privacy- and security-oriented product development.

Our motto, “Building & Connecting People with Privacy & Security,” reflects the standard we aim to bring to every product: useful functionality, thoughtful experiences and respect for the people who use technology.

Alongside our published Android apps, we are exploring SafeMobile, a security product initiative for Android. It remains in development while research and product work continue.
