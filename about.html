Our story

Technology that connects people, not compromises.

VibeSyncro Technologies Pvt Ltd is an India-based technology company focused on creating user-facing mobile applications and exploring research-led products in artificial intelligence, privacy and security. Our published Android applications address practical needs — from PDF workflows and developer settings shortcuts to creative drawing for children.

Alongside our current applications, we are researching SafeMobile, an Android security initiative with a privacy-first design direction. It remains in development. We do not claim that planned features are already available or that SafeMobile currently provides real-time antivirus or financial protection.

Our motto is Building & Connecting People with Privacy & Security. It reflects the standards we want to bring to every product: clear communication, respect for user choice, and responsible use of technology.
