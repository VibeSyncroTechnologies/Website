Privacy Policy

VibeSyncro Technologies Pvt Ltd · Last updated: October 2026

This policy describes the general privacy approach for this website and the Android applications published under VibeSyncro Technologies. Each app may have different features and technical integrations; review the relevant Google Play Data safety section and in-app notices for app-specific details.

1. Information We Collect

This static website does not provide user accounts or a server-side contact form. When you browse it, your browser and hosting provider may process standard technical data such as IP address, request time and device or browser information in order to deliver and secure the site. Our Android apps may process information needed to provide their respective features, permissions and services. The categories, purposes and retention can vary by app and version and are described in the applicable store disclosures and app notices.

2. How We Use Information

Information processed by a product may be used to provide and maintain its functions, respond to support requests, diagnose issues, protect the service, comply with legal obligations and improve reliability. We aim to follow data-minimisation principles and avoid collecting information that is not needed for a stated purpose.

3. Third-Party Services

Some products may rely on third-party platforms or services, including Google Play services and advertising or analytics providers where enabled. Their processing is governed by their own privacy terms. Check the app’s Google Play Data safety section and the notices presented within that app for the current integrations and details.

4. Advertising

The published apps identified on our Products page are described as containing ads. Google AdMob may be used as an advertising partner in supported app versions. Advertising providers may use device or advertising identifiers and related signals according to the user’s settings, applicable law and the provider’s policies. Ad personalisation and privacy controls may be available through Android and Google settings.

5. Children’s Privacy

One of our published apps is intended for young children. Parents and guardians should review its store disclosures, age rating, permissions and in-app experience before use. We do not intend this website to collect personal information directly from children. Any child-directed app must be assessed against the applicable child-safety, advertising and privacy requirements for the regions where it is distributed.

6. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures appropriate to the nature of the services we operate. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Specific app protections depend on the design and release of each app.

7. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your location and the applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, withdraw consent, restrict certain processing or raise a concern with a relevant authority. You can also manage Android app permissions and advertising preferences through your device settings. Requests relating to a particular app should identify the app and version concerned.

8. Retention

Retention periods depend on the purpose for which information is processed, the app’s features and applicable legal requirements. Data is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for those purposes, unless a longer period is required by law.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when products, services or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date. Material app-specific changes may also be communicated through the relevant app or store listing.
