Practices vary by app and version and are described in each app’s Google Play Data safety section and in-app notices.

Information may be used to provide app and website functionality, respond to requests, maintain reliability, diagnose issues, prevent abuse, protect services and meet legal obligations. We aim to apply data-minimisation principles and use information only for relevant purposes.

Depending on an app and its release, third-party services may support platform functionality, distribution, advertising, crash reporting or other features. Our published apps are identified as containing ads. Google AdMob may be used as an advertising partner in supported app versions. One published app is intended for young children. Parents and guardians should review its age rating, Data safety information, permissions, advertising disclosures and in-app experience before use. We do not knowingly use this company website to solicit personal information directly from children.
