Privacy Policy

VibeSyncro Technologies Pvt Ltd · Last updated: October 2026

This policy describes how VibeSyncro Technologies Pvt Ltd approaches information in connection with this website and the Android applications published under our developer account. Information practices can differ by app, feature and release version.

1. Information We Collect

This static website does not require an account. When you visit, website hosting and security systems may process standard technical information such as IP address, request time, browser type and device information to deliver and protect the site. Our Android apps may process information needed to operate their features, permissions and services. The information involved varies by app and version and is described in the relevant Google Play Data safety section and in-app notices.

2. How We Use It

Information may be used to provide app and website functionality, respond to requests, maintain reliability, diagnose issues, prevent abuse, protect services and meet legal obligations. We aim to apply data-minimisation principles and use information only for purposes relevant to the product or interaction.

3. Third-Party Services

Depending on the app and its release, third-party services may support distribution, core platform features, advertising, crash reporting or other functionality. These services may process information under their own privacy policies. Relevant integrations and disclosures should be reviewed in the applicable Google Play listing and within the app.

4. Advertising

The published applications described on our Products page are identified as containing ads. Google AdMob may be used as an advertising partner in supported app versions. Advertising services may process device identifiers, advertising identifiers, usage signals or other information as described by the provider and the app's disclosures. You can manage advertising and privacy settings through the controls available on your Android device and Google account.

5. Children's Privacy

One of our published apps is intended for young children. Parents and guardians should review the app's age rating, Data safety information, permissions, advertising disclosures and in-app experience before use. We do not knowingly use this company website to solicit personal information directly from children. Child-directed app features and services should be used with appropriate parental guidance and subject to applicable child-protection requirements.

6. Data Security

We seek to use reasonable technical and organisational measures appropriate to the nature of the services. No system or transmission over the internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Security protections depend on the product, device, operating system and app version.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of or deletion of personal information, withdraw consent, object to or restrict certain processing, or raise a concern with a relevant authority. You can also manage app permissions and advertising preferences through your device settings. Requests should identify the relevant app or service so they can be directed appropriately.

8. Retention and Changes

Information is retained for periods appropriate to the purpose for which it is processed, relevant product functions and legal requirements. We may update this policy as our products and services evolve. The current version and its updated date will be published on this page.
